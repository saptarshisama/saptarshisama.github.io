Monopsony

July 20, 2025

Monopsony:

  • One buyer rules the market — sellers dance to its tune.
  • Think of it as the mirror image of monopoly, but flipped around.
  • Classic example: a mining town with just one company hiring workers.
  • Good for the buyer's wallet, bad for everyone else's bargaining power.
  • It shows how power in markets isn't only about selling — it's also about buying.
